What it is

One system for the whole house

Home Assistant is an open-source home automation platform. It brings lighting, heating, blinds, cameras, doorbells, audio, energy and sensors from many different manufacturers into one app, one set of dashboards and one automation engine.

Unlike most smart home apps, it runs on a small dedicated device inside your home. Automations happen locally, so the lights still respond when the broadband is down, and camera footage and presence data don't have to leave the house.

It is also completely independent of any single manufacturer. If a brand discontinues a product or changes its app, you replace that device and everything else carries on.

Why homeowners choose it

  • Local control: fast, private and working without the internet.
  • Any brand: thousands of integrations, including Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread and Matter.
  • No compulsory subscription: an optional Home Assistant Cloud plan adds easy remote access and supports development.
  • Real automation: based on presence, time, weather, energy prices and sensors, not just schedules.
  • Yours to keep: open, documented and never tied to one installer.

Control4 integration

Home Assistant and Control4, working together

Many homes in Hertfordshire already have a Control4 system from an earlier AV or lighting installation. It does what it was set up to do well, but adding new devices can be expensive, some modern products aren't supported, and it may have been a while since anyone updated it.

You don't have to choose between them. Home Assistant can connect to your Control4 controller on the local network, bringing your Control4 lighting into Home Assistant alongside everything else. Where it makes sense, Control4 drivers can also expose Home Assistant devices to your Control4 touchscreens and remotes, so the family keeps the interface they know.

When a Control4 + Home Assistant setup makes sense

  • You want to add energy, EV charging, battery or solar control that Control4 doesn't cover.
  • You'd like affordable sensors (Zigbee motion, leak, door) without the cost of dealer-installed equipment.
  • You want richer automations or dashboards than your current programming offers.
  • Your original installer is no longer around and you want the system understood and documented.

Your Control4 system keeps running as it does today. Where changes to the Control4 programming itself are needed, we coordinate them with a Control4 dealer.

FAQ

Home Assistant questions

Does Home Assistant need an internet connection?

No. Home Assistant runs locally in your home, so automations and local devices keep working if the internet goes down. Internet is only needed for cloud-based devices, remote access and updates.

Is there a monthly subscription?

Home Assistant itself is free and open source. Home Assistant Cloud is an optional subscription that provides easy, secure remote access and voice assistant integrations while funding development. We can set up remote access either way, and our ongoing care plan is optional.

Can Home Assistant work with Control4?

Yes. Home Assistant has a Control4 integration that connects to your Control4 controller over the local network, and Control4 drivers can expose Home Assistant devices to Control4 interfaces. Your existing Control4 system keeps working as it does today.

Will it work with my existing smart devices?

Very likely. Home Assistant supports thousands of devices and services, including Philips Hue, Sonos, tado°, Hive, Nest, Ring, UniFi and anything using Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread or Matter. We'll confirm during the survey.

Can I keep using Alexa or Google Assistant?

Yes. Home Assistant can expose devices to Alexa, Google Assistant and Apple Home, or you can use its own private, local voice assistant.

What happens if you stop supporting my system?

Nothing breaks. Home Assistant is open source, your system is documented, and any competent person, including you, can take it over. There's no proprietary lock-in.

I built my own Home Assistant and it's become unreliable. Can you help?

Yes, this is one of the most common requests we get. We move it to reliable hardware, fix Zigbee and Wi-Fi issues, clean up automations and set up automatic backups, keeping as much of your existing work as possible.

How much does a Home Assistant installation cost?

It depends on the size of the property, the network and how many devices are involved. After a survey we provide a fixed written quote before any work starts.
