Start with the foundations

AI is only as safe as your permissions

AI assistants like Microsoft 365 Copilot can see everything the user can see. In most organisations that includes years of files shared with "everyone", forgotten SharePoint sites and mailboxes with loose permissions. Switch on AI without tidying that up and a casual question can surface salaries, HR cases or client contracts.

The second risk is shadow AI: staff pasting client data into free consumer chatbots because the business hasn't given them an approved alternative.

So every AI engagement starts with a readiness review: who can see what, where sensitive data lives, which tools people are already using, and what policy and controls are needed before anything is rolled out.

AI readiness review

  • Oversharing audit across SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams.
  • Sensitivity labels and DLP to keep confidential data out of AI answers.
  • Shadow AI assessment: which tools staff are using today.
  • AI acceptable use policy written for your business.
  • Licensing advice: who needs what, and who doesn't.

MCP integration

Connect AI to your business systems with MCP

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard for connecting AI assistants to tools and data. Instead of building a one-off integration for every AI product, a system is exposed once through an MCP server. Any compatible assistant can then use it, including Claude, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot Studio.

In practice, that means your team can ask an assistant to look up a customer in the CRM, check stock, raise a ticket or pull last month's figures, and it can do so from the live system rather than a stale export.

How we build MCP integrations

  • Least privilege: read-only by default, with write actions added deliberately.
  • Human approval for anything that changes data or sends something externally.
  • Per-user identity, so the AI can only reach what that person is allowed to see.
  • Logging of every request, so you can see exactly what the AI did and why.
  • Vetted servers only: we review or build MCP servers rather than installing unknown ones from the internet.

Systems we can connect

  • Microsoft 365: SharePoint, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive.
  • CRM and ATS platforms with an API.
  • Ticketing and helpdesk tools.
  • Accounting and ERP systems.
  • SQL databases and internal line-of-business apps.
  • File stores and document libraries.
Not sure if your system can connect? If it has an API, or a database we can safely read from, it can usually be connected.

FAQ

AI & automation questions

Is our data used to train AI models?

Microsoft states that Microsoft 365 Copilot doesn't use your organisation's data to train its foundation models, and business and API offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic don't train on your data by default. We use business-grade services and check the terms for each solution.

Does everyone need a Copilot licence?

No. We usually start with Copilot Chat, which is included with Microsoft 365, and a small pilot group on full licences. Usage data then shows where licences are worth paying for.

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to business systems and data through a consistent interface. It means one well-secured integration can work with several AI tools, including Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot Studio.

Should we use Power Automate or n8n?

If the process lives mostly in Microsoft 365, Power Automate is usually the right choice. For workflows across many third-party apps, or where self-hosting matters, n8n is often better. We recommend based on your systems, not a preference.

Are we too small for AI?

No. Smaller businesses often see the fastest returns, because a few hours saved each week is a large share of a small team's time. We start with one clear use case and prove the value before going further.

How long does a first project take?

A readiness review and a first pilot can typically be delivered in weeks rather than months, depending on the state of your data and permissions.

Which AI platforms do you work with?

Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio, Power Automate and the Power Platform, Claude, ChatGPT and Azure OpenAI, and n8n, Make and Zapier for workflow automation.
