The problem

Most small-business IT was never designed

It accumulated. A router from the broadband provider, a firewall nobody has logged into since installation, a Microsoft 365 tenant set up in an afternoon, shared passwords, and backups that have never been test-restored. It works, right up until someone clicks the wrong link or the one person who understands it leaves.

We take the approach that large organisations use: design it, secure it, document it, monitor it. Then we scale it to your size and your budget. The result is IT that your team stops noticing, and an estate that anyone competent could pick up from our documentation.

What a typical review turns up

  • Flat networks: guest Wi-Fi, CCTV and finance PCs all on one network.
  • MFA gaps: admin accounts and legacy mailboxes without multi-factor authentication.
  • Untested backups, or Microsoft 365 data with no backup at all.
  • Licence waste: paying for Business Premium seats nobody uses, or missing the features you pay for.
  • Unpatched devices and ex-staff accounts that were never disabled.

FAQ

IT support questions

How much does IT support cost?

It depends on the number of users, devices and sites, and on whether you want project work, ad-hoc support or a managed service. After a short review we give you a written, fixed proposal, so there are no surprises.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. We work on flexible terms with no lock-in. We'd rather keep clients because the service is good.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person or provider?

Yes. We often support an internal "IT person" with specialist networking, security or Microsoft 365 work, or take on a defined area from an existing provider.

Can you help us get Cyber Essentials?

We can review your setup against the Cyber Essentials technical controls (firewalls, secure configuration, access control, malware protection and patching), fix the gaps and prepare you for assessment.

What size of business do you support?

Typically businesses with between 1 and 50+ users, from single offices to multi-site operations. The approach scales; the standards don't change.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes. Most day-to-day issues are resolved remotely, with on-site visits across Hertfordshire for installation, hardware and anything that's better done in person.
